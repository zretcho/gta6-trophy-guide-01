    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone. The order below follows the list: story first, then the side strands, then the open-world grind, then the 100% cleanup that unlocks the gold A Long Road and the platinum Vice Got a Price! Story mission names are masked while the spoiler setting is on.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story

      Chapters 1 to 4, the final mission and the named story missions: Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory. Twelve trophies, all unmissable as you play — but keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (see Missables).

    2. Stage 2 — Side strands & partners

      Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, complete 8 stranger strands, and hunt the unique animal for Bret. Seven trophies. The finales stay open after the credits, so nothing here is time-critical except one race in Chapter 3 (see Strangers & Freaks).

    3. Stage 3 — Collectibles

      WAiNK It at 100 locations, 3 collectible card sets, meditation at all 18 spots, 100 vehicles discovered and all 52 areas of Leonida explored. Five trophies. Do the Chapter 1 meditation before that chapter ends — it is the list's one flagged missable.

    4. Stage 4 — Activities

      250 stunts, gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, a Top Fun drone race won at every one of the 14 locations, 20 different races won, 50 Challenges, all 9 activity types tried once, a fishing win over Cal, one arcade game finished, and the bowling alley. Nine trophies.

    5. Stage 5 — Robberies & money

      5 bank vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store robbed unseen, one illegal operation from each of the 9 gangs, $250,000 of marked bills laundered, $100,000 spent in shops, all 8 garages bought and 10 vehicles registered to the Scooter Bros app. Eleven trophies. Vaults and the jewelry tools open in Chapters 3 and 4.

    6. Stage 6 — Cleanup to 100%

      Finish the 100% checklist — 300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges, all 8 vaults, all 39 illegal operations — for A Long Road (gold), and the platinum follows. Around 200 hours in all, per the launch-day reporting.

    Missable trophies

    1 trophy is flagged as potentially missable: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation — Lucia on the balcony in Chapter 1 — disappears once that chapter ends. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. (PowerPyx's launch-day roadmap, as reported by Output Lag.)

    No other trophy is confirmed missable yet. Planet GTA 6 (19 Nov 2026) reports that no reliable missable list exists so far. Because the game has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, the safe habit is the same either way: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission. (Output Lag.)

    Cheats switch trophies off. Turning on a cheat disables trophies — and saving — so keep cheats away from your trophy save. (PowerPyx, as reported by Planet GTA 6; Rockstar confirmed the same rule for GTA V.)

    Sources & method

    Names, descriptions, grades and PSN progress counts are the official list as PlayStation publishes it, verified 19 November 2026 against the two trackers that mirror it — Exophase and PSNProfiles — which agree on every name, description and grade, and cross-checked with Planet GTA 6. Every guide is written in our own words for this page — sources are cited, never copied. Where no source pins a step down, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.
